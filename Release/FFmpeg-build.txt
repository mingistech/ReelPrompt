ReelPrompt uses FFmpeg 9.0.2 under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 2.1 or later. The unmodified corresponding FFmpeg source archive and license text are included in this folder.

FFmpeg is dynamically linked. No GPL or nonfree FFmpeg components were enabled.

FFmpeg build configuration:
./configure --prefix=/opt/reelprompt-ffmpeg --disable-autodetect --disable-everything --disable-static --enable-shared --disable-doc --disable-ffplay --disable-ffprobe --disable-network --enable-ffmpeg --enable-protocol=file,pipe --enable-demuxer=mov,matroska,avi,mpegts,flv,gif,image2 --enable-muxer=mp4 --enable-parser=h264,hevc,mpeg4video,vp8,vp9,av1,mpegvideo,mjpeg --enable-decoder=h264,hevc,mpeg4,vp8,vp9,av1,gif,mjpeg,prores,rawvideo,mpeg2video --enable-encoder=mpeg4 --enable-filter=scale,setsar,format --enable-swscale --install-name-dir=@rpath --extra-ldflags=-Wl,-rpath,@loader_path/../lib
make -j8
make install DESTDIR=<staging-directory>

Other bundled Python distributions include their license texts in their respective package and .dist-info directories. OpenCV distributes its FFmpeg libraries under LGPL v2.1; see opencv_python*.dist-info/METADATA. The Huihui model is downloaded separately; its terms are available at https://huggingface.co/divinetribe/Huihui-Qwen3-VL-32B-Instruct-abliterated-4bit-mlx.
